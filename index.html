A Decade in Miniature

Hot Wheels

I started collecting Hot Wheels in 2010 and, over the years, built a collection of more than 300 pieces, each with its own story and special place in my journey as a collector.

I stopped collecting in 2020, and this is the final car I purchased—the only one I ever had customized, by Milo Customs, as a personal tribute to a decade of collecting and the memories that came with it.

Ever Alone Remembered

There is no one in this world Who is dumb. Everyone has some special characteristics that are unique and great. We have come into this world alone, and we will also have to leave this world alone. Your studies, your work, your success all belong to you only. No one else will have a share in these. So, don't rest. Because if you reach the peak of your success, People will remember you for more than a thousand years.
