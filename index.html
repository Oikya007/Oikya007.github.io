About Me

My professional journey has been shaped by hospitality, sales, catering and event management — fields where relationships, attention to detail and memorable experiences matter.

Beyond my professional life, I have always been drawn toward creative expression. Photography allows me to stop and observe. Poetry allows me to turn thoughts into words. Collecting taught me that even small objects can carry stories and memories.

This space brings those different parts of my life together.

Hot Wheels

I started collecting Hot Wheels in 2010 and, over the years, built a collection of more than 300 pieces, each with its own story and special place in my journey as a collector.

I stopped collecting in 2020, and this is the final car I purchased—the only one I ever had customized, by Milo Customs, as a personal tribute to a decade of collecting and the memories that came with it.
